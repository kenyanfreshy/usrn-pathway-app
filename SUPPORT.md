# NurseBound support

NurseBound helps internationally educated nurses plan their move to the U.S., Canada, Australia or New Zealand
and prepare for the NCLEX-RN, OET and IELTS.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?** No. Everything is saved on your phone.

**I got a new phone. How do I keep my progress?** On the old phone, open More → Profile & settings → Backup & restore →
Save a backup. On the new phone, open the same screen, tap Restore a backup and paste the text.

**How do I restore my Pro purchase?** More → NurseBound Pro → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account
you bought with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?** iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → NurseBound. Android: Google Play →
Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

**Is NurseBound affiliated with NCSBN, OET, IELTS or a government?** No. It is an independent study and planning app.
Always confirm requirements on the official sites linked in the app.
