**Description:**
USRN Pathway guides internationally educated nurses from credential evaluation to the bedside in the United States, Canada, Australia or New Zealand — and beyond.

ROADMAP & DOCUMENTS
• Pick your destination: a 6-phase roadmap for the U.S., Canada, Australia or New Zealand, with official links and fees for each step
• U.S.: credentials, state board, NCLEX-RN, English test, VisaScreen and immigration
• A personal timeline with your estimated arrival date
• A document vault with expiry tracking for IELTS/PTE scores and licenses
• A cost tracker for every fee you pay

NCLEX-RN PRACTICE
• 600+ NCLEX-RN questions with rationales for every answer choice, including select-all-that-apply and ordered-response
• Three benchmark exams: an 85-question Diagnostic, a 150-item NGN Clinical Judgment Simulator and an adaptive Comprehensive Predictor (85–150 questions)
• All NCLEX-RN client-needs areas, including maternal-newborn, pediatrics, mental health and high-acuity care
• Next Generation NCLEX (NGN) case studies with chart tabs, SATA, matrix, highlight, drop-down and ordered-response items
• Tutor and exam modes, flashcards, and save-and-resume for long exams

VISA & SPONSORS
• U.S.: EB-3 visa bulletin dates and a priority-date wait estimate
• Canada: Express Entry healthcare draws and provincial nominee streams
• Australia: SkillSelect points, nursing occupations and state nomination
• New Zealand: Green List Straight to Residence and the Accredited Employer Work Visa
• Directories of ethical recruiters and hospital employers for each country

U.S. BEDSIDE & CAREER
• Lessons on SBAR, code status, delegation and documentation
• Practice notes for Canada, Australia and New Zealand
• Defensible charting practice and license-protection guidance
• Guides to health, life, disability, car and home insurance, credit building and driving

PRO (optional — 2-week free trial for new subscribers, then a monthly subscription; or a one-time pass)
• NGN Clinical Judgment Simulator • Expiry reminders on your phone • Endorsement wizard between states
• Charge nurse tools: acuity balancer, SBAR scripts, clinical ladder log • Overtime and tax calculators

Your data stays on your phone. No account needed.

USRN Pathway is for education only. It is not legal, immigration, medical or financial advice.
