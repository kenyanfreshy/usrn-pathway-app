## What stays on your phone
Everything you enter — your name, profile details, roadmap progress, documents list and dates, quiz results, notes and calculator
inputs — is stored only on your device. We do not run a server and we cannot see this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Reminders
If you turn on expiry reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone. They are created and shown by your device; no
information leaves it.

## Purchases
Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store or Google Play. They process your payment; we never see your card or bank details.
We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether your account has an active Pro purchase. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and a
random app user ID (not your name or email). See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## What we don't do
We don't sell data, show ads, use tracking, or share information with data brokers.

## Children
The app is intended for adult nurses and is not directed to children under 13.
